Expert WiFi Safety Guide

Public WiFi Safety

  • Use a VPN to encrypt your device-to-server tunnel.
  • Only enter data on "HTTPS" websites with padlock icons.
  • Disable "Auto-Connect" to prevent joining rogue hotspots.
  • Activate OS firewalls before connecting to shared APs.

Home Hardening

  • Rotate Default Credentials: Change factory admin logins.
  • Disable WPS/UPnP to close legacy exploit entry points.
  • Firmware Management: Regularly update router software.
  • Isolate IoT devices on a separate Guest VLAN/Network.
